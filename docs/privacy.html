Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

What data is used

When the owner connects their TikTok account, frightclub receives an access token and basic account information (open ID, display name, avatar) from TikTok, and the information needed to upload videos (creator privacy options, upload status).

How it is used

This data is used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account and to show the upload status. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Storage

Access tokens are stored encrypted in the tool's server environment (Cloudflare Workers) and are deleted when access is revoked. Video files are kept only as long as needed to complete the upload.

Your control

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Security → Manage app permissions). After revocation the stored token is deleted.
